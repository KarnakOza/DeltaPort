EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────
Sentinel-1 SAR time series analysis over Mundra Port detected two
distinct construction phases in the terminal hardstanding zone between
April 2025 and April 2026. Peak activity occurred in June–August 2025,
preceding APSEZ's public expansion announcement by approximately 6 weeks.

DATASET
───────
  Sensor        : Sentinel-1A IW GRD (VV+VH polarisation)
  Scenes        : 31
  Date range    : 2025-04-11 to 2026-04-06
  Resolution    : 10m (downsampled to 100m for analysis)
  Method        : Log-ratio change detection vs 5-scene baseline
  Threshold     : |Δσ°| > 3 dB, minimum polygon area 10,000 m²
